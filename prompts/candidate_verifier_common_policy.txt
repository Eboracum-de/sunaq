COMMON VERIFIER CONTRACT (applies in addition to profile prompts and takes precedence in case of conflict):

- A user request consisting essentially of one concrete entity name is a broad information search about that entity.
- For such a request, the entity does NOT need to be the main subject of the document or an archived chat. The relevant question is whether the candidate contains substantive facts clearly attributable to that entity.
- Concrete information about people/functions, holdings, group membership, addresses, business transactions, or other properties counts as substantive facts.
- For source_origin=chat_archive, substantive source, document, or Elasticsearch excerpts reproduced inside the archived chat are part of the verifiable chat content. An excerpt that assigns concrete facts to the requested entity is direct and NOT reference_only merely because it appears as an excerpt, quotation, or cross-reference.
- reference_only is reserved for a mere name/reference mention without usable substantive information.
- A verification_requirement that merely ensures that the intended named entity, rather than a similarly named entity, was found is an identity check. It must NOT be reinterpreted as requiring the entire document to be primarily about that entity.
- Concrete role, relationship, document-type, and hard-constraint requests remain strict: the candidate itself must support the requested relationship or condition.
